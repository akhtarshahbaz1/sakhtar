## Part D: Content Strategy That Sells

### YouTube Content Plan

| Day | Content Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Full tutorial (15-30 min) | Watch time + AdSense |
| Wednesday | Shorts - Quick C# tip | Reach + subscribers |
| Friday | Project build / Live coding | Engagement + course promotion |
| Saturday | Shorts - Career advice | Broad audience |

**Best Performing Tech Content in India (Hindi):**
- "How I earn X lakhs as a developer" (aspirational)
- "Build X project from scratch" (practical)
- "X vs Y - which to learn?" (decision-making)
- "Interview questions + answers" (evergreen)
- "My developer setup/tools" (relatable)

### Instagram Content Plan

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Reels - coding tips (30-60 sec) | Daily | Reach + followers |
| Carousel - cheat sheets/tutorials | 3x/week | Saves + shares |
| Stories - behind the scenes | Daily | Connection + trust |
| Story polls/questions | 2x/week | Engagement + product ideas |
| Product launch posts | As needed | Sales |

### Content Ideas Specific to Your Advantage

1. **"Bhadohi se coding"** - Small town developer story resonates HUGELY in India
2. **Building in public** - Show your SaaS development journey
3. **Local business digitization** - Help Bhadohi carpet shops go online (great story)
4. **Tier-2/3 city developer life** - Relatable to 80% of Indian developers
