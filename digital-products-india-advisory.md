## Legal Requirements for Selling Digital Products in India

### Mandatory

1. **PAN Card** - Required for any business activity
2. **GST Registration** - Required if turnover exceeds 20 lakh/year (for services)
   - Below 20 lakh: Optional but recommended for credibility
   - Digital products to Indian customers: 18% GST
3. **Bank Account** - Business/current account recommended but savings works initially

### Recommended

4. **MSME/Udyam Registration** - Free, gives you government benefits and credibility
   - Register at: udyamregistration.gov.in
   - Benefits: lower interest rates, tax benefits, government tender preference
5. **Business Structure:**
   - **Start as:** Sole Proprietorship (simplest, no registration cost)
   - **Scale to:** LLP or Private Limited when revenue grows
6. **Digital Product Compliance:**
   - No special license needed for selling software/digital products
   - Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 apply
   - Display: legal name, address, contact details, refund policy on your website

### Tax Implications

| Income Level | Tax Treatment |
|---|---|
| Under 7 lakh/year | Zero tax under new regime (Section 87A rebate) |
| 7-10 lakh/year | 10% tax (new regime) |
| SaaS to foreign clients | Export of services - 0% GST (with LUT filing) |
| Freelance/consulting income | File ITR-3 or ITR-4 (presumptive taxation under 44ADA) |

**Key Tax Advantage:** If you sell SaaS to international clients, it qualifies as "Export of Services" - you pay 0% GST and can claim input tax credit.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't build in isolation** - Talk to 10 potential customers before writing code
2. **Don't over-engineer** - MVP first, architecture later
3. **Don't ignore Hindi market** - 500M+ Hindi speakers online, underserved in tech
4. **Don't compete with free** - Add value that free tools don't provide (support, customization, compliance)
5. **Don't skip legal basics** - Get PAN, GST, and Udyam registration early
6. **Don't ignore SEO** - Most Indian small business owners Google their problems in Hindi
7. **Don't price too low** - Indian SaaS buyers respect value; 299/month is fine for a useful tool
